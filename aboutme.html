Nice to meet you!

A quick resume of my history so far. (Projects will be better described on its page!)

Hi! My name is Gustavo Hideki. I am 21 and currently living in Munich. I am Brazilian, from the city of São Paulo, where I lived most of my life until moving to Germany.

My main passions are tech and cars, though I consider myself a person of many interests. In a broader sense, I am very detailist, so I appreciate everything that took a good amount of effort to create. For example, after moving out of my parents' home, I started to really get into cooking and learning the fundamental recipes. I also like reading self-development and learning-focused books, and I am now planning to start publishing some of my notes online, drawn from classes, books, and past experiences (both to share with and to learn from other people).

In my home country, I did a bachelor's degree (BSc) at Escola Politécnica da Universidade de São Paulo (POLI-USP), where I took part in the Formula SAE team, Equipe Poli Racing. I joined the team in my very first semester and worked with them for 3.5 years, going from trainee to vice-captain. Needless to say, I fell in love with the project.

Today, I am studying for a Master's degree in Automotive Engineering at the Technical University of Munich (TUM) in a double degree program with my home university. In parallel, I am also working at Hexagon Sports, a Brazilian startup focused on developing the biggest sports social media platform on the internet. Right now, our main product is the Highlight Engine, which lets you easily capture the best moments of your games. Since this is a different field from my degree, I am starting by deepening my coding and software knowledge (beginning with CS50x, which is what this website is being built for).

Last but not least, I am part of Fundação Estudar, the most competitive scholarship program in Brazil, where a community of more than 1,000 fellows is shaping the next innovations and development of the country (and of the world). There, I met some of the most brilliant minds I have ever encountered, which broadened my perspective in a way I cannot describe.

As for the next steps, I am committed to helping Hexagon grow into the next Brazilian unicorn, while also looking for opportunities at the intersection of the automotive field and cutting-edge technology (or even all of them combined!).
